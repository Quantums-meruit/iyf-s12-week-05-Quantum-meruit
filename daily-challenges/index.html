Daily DOM Challenges

Five small exercises in selecting, creating and updating page elements.

Day 1 — Color Changer

Change every h1, h2 and h3 heading to a randomly generated hex color.

Day 2 — Dynamic Element Creator

Day 3 — Image Remover

Remove all images, or use the bonus control to hide and show them instead.

Copy the source content into the target. Bonus: drag the source box into the target.
